### Namaste Chai

This was one of my favourites in college, and I was worried I'd set some high expectations if I started with this one. But it was already in my inventory, so I proceeded with testing it.

Namaste Chai always has a very strong aroma for every flavour. You might find this too much, but I personally found it calming as I had _chai_ after a long day of classes/work.
