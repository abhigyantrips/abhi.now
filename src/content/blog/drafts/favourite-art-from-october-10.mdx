This collection was originally much larger than it is right now, but I learned that a lot of the posts I saved have been taken down since under the State's rigorous banishing of free speech online. You can read [the Internet Freedom Foundation's coverage](https://internetfreedom.in/meitys-enhanced-diligence-advisory-pressures-platforms-to-police-protest-speech-it-must-be-withdrawn/) on this.

They ironically call it "enhanced diligence", which is something _they_ could have shown when crimes of rape and abuse became rampant all across the country.
