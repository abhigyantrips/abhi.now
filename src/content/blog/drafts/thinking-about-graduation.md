By all technical means, I have already graduated. I have given my final presentation and done the exit form shenanigans, and received some form of paper from the college that says, "You have done the academic work. We will let you know the rest later."

I have already been a working individual for over 6 months, thanks to how engineering colleges work. It's good for my growth after college, which [I've already begun to experience](/weeknotes/2026/35) after converting. But I also think it blurred the line between college ending and work starting for me, which led me to being surprised when my final project presentation completed and it was just... over.

[a picture from college life]

I remember every single year of college feeling like years long. I would pack up all my stuff after the finals, say goodbye to all my friends and take a bittersweet flight home. The two-month break would feel like its own season, and I'm sure my parents saw me change with every visit I made back dragging a suitcase behind me.

Every year was a new set of roommates for me, and it took two years of CGPA grinding for me to get one of my best friends (Satyajit) as a roommate[^1].

#### Footnotes

[^1]: Our hostels allocated rooms (and roommate preferences) based on the CGPA of the students making a request. Both the room type (double/triple) and choice of roommate depended on your academic merit.
