For both our sakes, I'm not going to italicize _chai_ for the rest of the post. We both know I'm referring to tea, so let's keep it that way.

### Namaste Chai

This was one of my favourites in college, and I was worried I'd set some high expectations if I started with this one. But it was already in my inventory, so I proceeded with testing it.

Namaste Chai always has a very strong aroma for every flavour. You might find this too much, but I personally found it calming as I had chai after a long day of classes/work.

### Chaayos

### Red Label

I kinda like Red Label as a brand, in a purely aesthetic sense. I mentioned in a previous blogpost that our family was trying out their _chaipatti_ as an alternative to Taj Mahal, which is still an experiment in progress. But I was excited to have their chai premix.

After making the first cup and drinking it without an accompaniment (biscuits or otherwise), I was left frowning at my mug. The colour and smell were fine, but the taste itself felt like it was _artificially sweetened_.

And not even in a way that would improve the chai experience! The concoction tasted how those yellow SugarFree&trade; powders used to smell. I was quite disappointed by this one.

### TATA Tea Gold

I _think_ there's something wrong with this one? The chai itself is nice, and doesn't have any noticeable amount of sugar. But every time I make this chai, I've noticed there's a ring of red residue left at the top of the mug.

I've made sure it's not something that was already in the mug. From what I've observed though, it's the foam from the premix that first starts out orange and then later on shifts to red. I don't know if this points to how real or synthetic the premix is, but I'm probably not going to buy this one again. Ever.
